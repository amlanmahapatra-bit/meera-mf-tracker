Trackerr – Components Map

A weekly mutual-fund check-up for Meera, in plain words. All numbers are worked out by ordinary code. The AI (Google Gemini Flash only) just explains them – and its answer is checked before anyone sees it.

Live site: https://trackerr-amlan-mahapatra-1955.vercel.appCode: https://github.com/amlanmahapatra-bit/meera-mf-tracker
Every day · 7:00 am ISTSaves the latest fund prices and index levels
Every Saturday · 7:30 am ISTBuilds that week’s report and emails it with a PDF
Sun–Tue · safety netIf Saturday’s email was missed, builds and sends it again (never twice)
Any time · by hand“Update now” button; Meera adds or removes a fund (passcode, no code changes)
1. Get live datafrom free public sources
mfapi.inDaily price (NAV) of every fund, full history. Also used to search funds by name.
Yahoo FinanceNifty 50, Nifty Midcap 150, Nifty Smallcap 250, Nasdaq 100 and the dollar–rupee rate.
GNewsThis week’s India, economy and world market headlines.Backup: Google News RSS, switches on automatically
2. Save itSupabase database
Prices & index historyEvery fund price and index close, saved so the site still works if a source is down.
Your 5 fundsWhich funds Meera tracks, with their market index and similar funds. Add or remove any time.
Weekly snapshots & reportsEvery Saturday saved – 5 years of weeks – for the trend chart.
Run log & email logEvery job’s start, end and result; every email sent. Jobs are safe to repeat – nothing is duplicated.Only our server can read it (no public access)
3. Do the mathsplain code – no AI, fully tested
ReturnsWeek, month, year-so-far, and 1 day to 5 years. Weekends and holidays use the last price before.
CompareFund vs its market index and vs the average of similar funds, on the same dates.
“Market or fund?” splitHow much of the week’s move came from the whole market, and how much from the fund itself.
Traffic light & verdictGreen / amber / red per fund. Overall: “No action needed”, “One fund worth watching” or “Consider reviewing your allocation”, with a reason.
4. Explain in plain wordsGemini Flash via OpenRouter – the only AI
Gemini writes the words3 short lines per fund (what happened, how it compares, why – from a real headline), a longer story, and a market summary.
CheckerRejects invented numbers, jargon, buy/sell advice and made-up headlines. Up to 3 tries.
Template fallbackIf Gemini is down or keeps failing, code writes the same report from the numbers. A report always exists.
5. Deliver to Meerawhat she actually sees
Website (Vercel)Dashboard with verdict and 5 traffic-light cards · fund pages with 5-year price charts · “How it works”.
Weekly report pageThe whole week on one printable page.
Saturday email + PDF (Resend)The same detailed report, with a PDF attached.
“Ask about my money” chatAnswers her questions using only her saved report and the week’s headlines.
Add / remove a fund formSearch by name or paste a scheme code. Passcode protected.

Built so it never breaks – and so the numbers can be trusted

Source down? Uses the saved copy and says “data as of” – never a blank page
News source down? Switches to Google News automatically
AI down or wrong? Checker + template, so the report still goes out
Email failed? Re-sent Sun–Tue, and never sent twice
Every job is logged with its status (visible at /api/health)
Secrets live only in Vercel settings; timers need a secret, fund changes need a passcode
Colour key: Outside data Database Our code AI (Gemini Flash) What Meera sees
Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase and Vercel · Honest limits: no free benchmark exists for the debt fund (compared with similar funds only) and Nifty Smallcap 100 has no free data (Smallcap 250 is used).
